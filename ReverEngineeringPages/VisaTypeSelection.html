Schengen Visa - Previous Visa Holder
This category is applicable only for previous Schengen visa holders and you have to enter your visa details as a mandatory requirement.
CASA 1
-Applicants who have never previously obtained a Schengen visa.
-Applicants who obtained a Schengen visa before January 2020.
CASA 2
- People who have obtained a Schengen visa for a maximum of 2 years issued from 01-01-2020.
- People who have obtained a Schengen visa for between 2 and 3 years issued in 2020.
CASA 3
- Holders of a Schengen visa valid for between 3 and 5 years, issued by any Schengen State from 01-01-2020, which has expired or will expire in the next 3 months.
- Holders of a Schengen visa valid for between 2 and 3 years, issued by any Schengen State on or after 01-01-2021, which has expired or will expire within the next 3 months.
RABAT 1

RABAT 2

RABAT 3
People who have a single visa or other visas from Schengen countries.
